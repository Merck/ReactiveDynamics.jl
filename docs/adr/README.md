ADRs 0007–0012 are the Phase-0.5 modeling-language extension increment (2026-06-21), answering the maintainer's business-process modeling asks: a refined/compact/compositional metalanguage with substitutable granularity (0009), a contracted interface with declarative + dumpable initial state (0007), filtration-based selection of agentic-species tokens (0008), an endogenous, state-contingent decision channel — in-model rules/callbacks/conditional transitions — that makes an expressive BD demo possible (0010), full action expressivity — population-level token-field writes plus a general-code escape hatch through the eval-free registry — so callbacks can run arbitrary queries/code without reopening the RCE (0011), and AlgebraicAgents interoperability — a reactive network as a node in the AA hierarchy that exposes its state outbound and reads sibling/parent state inbound via declared wired ports (0012). They map to CONTRACT §10, §11, §9.5, §12, the §12.3/§8.4 amendment, and §13 respectively. ADRs 0013–0014 are the Phase-0.6 analysis & visualization increment (2026-06-29) — the RESULT-inspection layer answering "inspect an ensemble of runs, gather ledger statistics, extract typical agentic-token trajectories, export, plot, and draw a result-decorated network exec map for (in)efficiency analysis." They sit ON TOP of the §1–§13 modeling layer without disturbing it (read-only over a finished run), map to CONTRACT §14 and §15, and promote the [MVP_BD_DEMO.md](../MVP_BD_DEMO.md) demo's hand-rolled `analysis.jl`/`figures.jl` into engine APIs. 0013's ensemble member-REINIT mode is gated on the ADR 0007 §D `_reinit!` completion; both reuse existing infrastructure (the per-program ledger seam, AA's `getobservable`/`run_graphviz`/`wiring_diagram`) rather than adding dependencies. 0007–0009 and 0012 are PROPOSED pending sign-off — every `file:line` is read-verified against `ref-agents` (and, for 0012, against the AlgebraicAgents source), but unlike 0001–0006 they have not yet had an adversarial running-engine verification pass. 0010 and 0011 are maintainer-ratified (2026-06-21) and were driven by the [MVP_BD_DEMO.md](../MVP_BD_DEMO.md) exercise; 0010's repair of the event channel (`solvers.jl:323`) and 0011's reuse of the ADR-0006 §C registry boundary are verified against source.
